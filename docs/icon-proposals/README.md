# Marketplace icon proposals

These are preview-only SVG concepts. The existing `icon.png` is unchanged.

- `01-rose-seal.svg` — simplified rose-window seal; strongest at small sizes.
- `02-cathedral-lancet.svg` — pointed cathedral window with a blue flame; more architectural.
- `03-faceted-blue.svg` — abstract stained-glass facets forming a subtle C; most modern.
- `01-rose-seal-v2.svg` — rose seal without the diagonal split; more coherent and emblematic.
- `02-cathedral-lancet-v2.svg` — lancet without the diagonal split; a calmer, more symmetrical version.
- `02-cathedral-lancet-v3.svg` — keeps the two-tone water-drop idea, but confines the diagonal to the lancet silhouette.
- `03-faceted-blue-v2.svg` — replaces the round gold eye with a structural gold lozenge at the facet junction.
- `03-faceted-blue-v3.svg` — baroque iteration: nested tracery, gold scrollwork, jewel pins, and an integrated faceted center.
- `03-faceted-blue-v4.svg` — single-plane mosaic revision; ornament is expressed only through symmetric pane divisions and colour rhythm.
- `03-faceted-blue-v5.svg` — eight-way balanced revision; repeated petal units distribute detail evenly across all sides.

## 08 variants — fixing the muddy red and green

`08-chandelier-layers.svg` has the strongest composition, but its red and green read as dirt
rather than as colour. The reason is luminance: ruby is 0.045 and green 0.12 in relative
luminance, both hugging the `#0E1B33` field (0.013), while the gold they sit beside is 0.41.
The eye gets one low grey band instead of a red and a green. Three ways out of that band:

- `08a-jewel-band.svg` — keeps both hues and lifts them into the gold's band (green 0.28,
  ruby 0.18). Everything else is byte-identical to 08.
- `08b-teal-ember.svg` — trades the red-against-green clash for cool-against-warm: teal ring,
  ember-orange crown teeth. Both amber bands brighten too, since fresh teal next to `#A05810`
  greys out for the same reason the red did.
- `08c-cobalt-gold.svg` — no second hue family at all: azure ring, crown alternating gold with
  the deep blue it already sits on, so the dark crown tooth reads as deliberate inlay.
